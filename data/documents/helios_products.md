# Helios Analytics - Product Catalogue 2026

Helios Analytics (a fictional company) sells three software products.

## InsightHub (code: HX-100)
InsightHub is a self-service business-intelligence dashboard platform. It connects to SQL databases, spreadsheets and cloud warehouses.
- Price: 450 USD per month for up to 10 users; each additional user costs 35 USD per month.
- Launched: 2021.
- Key features: drag-and-drop dashboards, scheduled reports, row-level security.
- Typical customers: retail and FMCG companies.

## DocuMind (code: DM-220)
DocuMind is an enterprise document-intelligence product built on Agentic RAG. It ingests PDFs, Word files and emails, and lets employees ask questions in Arabic or English with cited answers.
- Price: 1,200 USD per month, including 50,000 queries; extra queries cost 0.02 USD each.
- Launched: 2024.
- Key features: hybrid search, multi-step reasoning agent, source citations, on-premise deployment option.
- Typical customers: banks, law firms and government agencies.

## ForecastPro (code: FP-310)
ForecastPro is a demand-forecasting service that uses time-series machine-learning models.
- Price: 800 USD per month per business unit.
- Launched: 2022.
- Key features: automatic model selection, holiday effects for the MENA region, what-if scenarios.
- Typical customers: supply-chain and manufacturing companies.

## Support plans
All products include Standard support (business hours, 24-hour response). Premium support costs an extra 20% of the product price and offers a 4-hour response time and a dedicated account manager.
